== Installation ==

1. Go to **Plugins > Add New** in your WordPress admin.
2. Search for **Link Building** or **AI SEO**.
3. Click **Install Now** and then **Activate**.
4. Navigate to the 'LexonRank' menu to watch your AI backlink stats grow.
